About the Project

Motorcycle Racing Championship Management System

Bike Race Champion is a desktop application for managing motorcycle racing championships efficiently. Administrators can manage riders, teams, countries, seasons, races, registrations, results, and standings, while users can register for races, track their status, view standings, and use the AI Race Assistant.
